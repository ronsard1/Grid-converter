How it works

Uses the paper-ruler method from your printed map:

  • 2.0 cm = 1 km grid square
  • 0.2 cm = 100 m subdivision
  • 18.5 cm = 5 arc-minutes
  • 1 grid unit ≈ 3.2432″

Each profile anchors to its own origin. No projection math.
